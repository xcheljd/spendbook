# Spendbook Product Guide

## Core Purpose
Spendbook is a manual expense tracking application for iPhone designed for individuals who value privacy and seek radical self-accountability. Unlike traditional apps that automate tracking via bank sync, Spendbook intentionally utilizes manual entry to create a moment of reflection and friction, helping users break bad spending habits and maintain strict control over their financial data.

## Target Audience
- **Privacy Advocates:** Users who refuse to link their bank accounts to third-party apps and prefer keeping their financial data local or within their private iCloud environment.
- **Habit Builders:** People seeking to curb impulsive spending through the deliberate act of logging every transaction manually.

## Key Features
- **Rapid Manual Entry:** A streamlined, frictionless entry process optimized for speed (widgets/shortcuts) to ensure consistent logging without unnecessary overhead.
- **Visual Accountability:** Support for photo capture per entry, allowing users to keep a visual record of their purchases.
- **Dynamic Tagging System:**
    - Hierarchical tags (e.g., "Food > Dining Out") for granular tracking.
    - Color-coded tags for instant visual recognition in the spending feed.
    - Smart suggestions based on frequency and recent usage.
- **Accountability Goals:**
    - "Safe-to-Spend" Metrics: Daily/weekly allowance views to provide positive reinforcement.
    - Spending limits with monthly/weekly caps.
    - Behavioral streaks to track "no-spend" days and consistent logging habits.
- **Advanced Filtering:** Powerful search and filter capabilities based on tags, categories, and presence of photo attachments.
- **Local Intelligence:** On-device OCR to scan receipts for amount/date, requiring user confirmation to maintain the "accountability moment" without the tedium of typing.
- **Zero-Barrier Onboarding:** No account creation or login required to start using the app.

## Data & Privacy
- **Local-First Architecture:** Data is stored and encrypted on-device.
- **Biometric Lock:** Optional Privacy Shield using FaceID or TouchID to prevent unauthorized access to financial records.
- **Private Sync:** Optional iCloud synchronization to keep data consistent across the user's Apple devices without external servers.
- **Data Sovereignty:** Full support for exporting data in CSV or JSON formats for personal analysis.
