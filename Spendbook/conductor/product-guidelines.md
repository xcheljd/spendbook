# Product Guidelines

## Visual Identity & Design Language
- **Core Aesthetic:** Sleek, modern, and compact. Inspired by **shadcn/ui**, featuring clean lines, thoughtful use of whitespace, and highly legible typography.
- **Color Palette:** **Catppuccin** (Adaptive Theme).
    - **Dark Mode (Default):** **Mocha** (Deepest charcoal/black base). Maximizes OLED efficiency and provides a high-contrast, focused environment.
    - **Light Mode:** **Latte** (Warm, soft light base). Provides a cozy, low-strain reading experience for daylight usage.
    - **Accents:** Vibrant but soft pastels (Blue, Sapphire, Lavender, Mauve) consistent across both themes for tags, charts, and interactive elements.
- **Components:** Modular, card-based layouts. Inputs should feel substantial but minimal.
- **Atmosphere:** Calm and efficient. The design should lower the user's heart rate, not raise it.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Efficiency First:** Every interaction should be optimized for speed. Minimizing taps to log an expense is the "north star" metric.
- **Clarity:** Data visualization (charts, lists) should be dense but decipherable. Use the "compact" nature of the design to show more context without clutter.
- **Frictionless Entry:** Use smart defaults and predictive inputs to make manual entry feel almost magical.

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Personality:** Playful, personified, and engaging.
- **Tone:** Lighthearted and game-like.
- **Approach:** Turn the chore of budgeting into a small daily quest. Use humor or friendly nudges when limits are approached, rather than harsh warnings. Celebrate streaks and "safe-to-spend" days with genuine enthusiasm.
